Silva Navigation Quickstart

1. Drag the compass baseplate to position the pivot over your starting point.

2. Rotate the red Dial bezel (using the bottom-right wheel) to align the baseplate orientation with your target line of travel.

3. Look at the top center of the compass dial to read your bearing.

4. Dynamic rulers match the map scale automatically when zooming, allowing easy grid-coordinate distance tracking.
